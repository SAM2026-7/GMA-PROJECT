Counseling Program

Walking with you through every season of life.

Our Counseling Program offers confidential, faith-based guidance to help individuals, couples, and families navigate life's challenges. Led by experienced pastors and trained counselors, each session is rooted in biblical principles and tailored to your unique needs.

Types of Counseling Sessions

Marriage Counseling

Strengthen your marriage through pre-marital preparation and relationship enrichment sessions.

Family Counseling

Resolve family conflicts, improve communication, and build a loving, godly household.

Youth Counseling

Guidance for young people navigating faith, academics, career, and peer relationships.

Pastoral Consultation

One-on-one spiritual guidance for personal decisions, calling, and life direction.

Career & Purpose Counseling

Clarity on career choices, business decisions, and discovering God's purpose for your life.

Follow-up Sessions

Continued support and accountability after initial counseling to ensure lasting progress.

Who Can Request Counseling?

Counseling is available to all members of GMA City Complex and visitors. Whether you are a first-time guest or a long-standing member, our doors are open. We welcome individuals of all ages seeking biblical guidance and emotional support. Sessions can be booked online, by phone, or in person at the church office.

🔒
Confidentiality Statement
All counseling sessions at GMA City Complex are strictly confidential. Information shared during sessions is protected and will never be disclosed without your explicit written consent. Our counselors adhere to the highest ethical and pastoral standards to safeguard your privacy and trust.

Healing Program

Experiencing God's restorative power through faith and prayer.

Our Healing Program is dedicated to ministering spiritual and emotional healing through the power of faith, prayer, and the Word of God. We believe in the biblical promise of divine healing and provide a safe, compassionate environment for those seeking restoration.

Types of Healing Sessions

Spiritual Healing

Freedom from spiritual oppression, strongholds, and deep-seated spiritual battles through prayer and deliverance.

Emotional Healing

Recovery from trauma, grief, depression, anxiety, and emotional wounds through pastoral care and God's Word.

Physical Healing Prayer

Faith-based prayer for physical ailments and health challenges, trusting God for divine intervention.

Inner Healing & Deliverance

Deep inner healing sessions addressing past hurts, bitterness, and generational patterns through the Holy Spirit.

Grief & Loss Recovery

Compassionate support for those mourning the loss of a loved one or processing significant life changes.

Healing Prayer Nights

Corporate healing prayer gatherings held monthly, open to all who seek God's restorative touch.

Who Can Request Healing Prayer?

Healing prayer is available to everyone — members, visitors, friends, and family. You do not need to be a registered member to receive healing ministry. If you or someone you know is going through a physical, emotional, or spiritual health challenge, we invite you to come forward in faith. Healing sessions can also be conducted over the phone or via video call for those who are unable to attend in person.

🔒
Confidentiality Statement
Your healing journey is personal and sacred. All information shared during healing sessions is treated with the utmost confidentiality. We maintain strict privacy protocols and will never share your details without your written permission. You are safe here.

Prayer Program

Covering you and your needs in fervent, effective prayer.

Our Prayer Program provides structured, dedicated prayer support for individuals, families, and the wider community. From personal prayer sessions to corporate intercession, we are committed to standing with you before God in prayer for every area of your life.

Types of Prayer Sessions

Personal Prayer Session

One-on-one prayer with a pastor or intercessor for your specific personal needs and burdens.

Family Prayer Session

Corporate prayer for your entire family covering unity, protection, provision, and spiritual growth.

Intercessory Prayer

Our dedicated prayer teams stand in the gap for you, praying consistently and persistently for your breakthrough.

Breakthrough Prayer

Intense, focused prayer sessions targeting specific breakthroughs in career, finances, health, and relationships.

Morning Prayer Vigil

Join us for early morning prayer sessions to start your day with spiritual covering and divine direction.

Corporate Prayer Gatherings

Monthly prayer meetings where the entire church comes together to seek God for the community and nation.

Who Can Request Prayer?

Prayer is for everyone. You do not need to be a member of GMA City Complex to submit a prayer request or join a prayer session. We welcome individuals, families, organizations, and communities seeking divine intervention. Prayer requests can be submitted online, through the prayer wall, by phone, or by visiting the church office. Private prayer requests are kept strictly confidential.

🔒
Confidentiality Statement
All prayer requests are handled with care and reverence. Private requests are shared only with designated prayer team members and are never disclosed publicly. You can trust that your prayer needs are covered in a safe and supportive environment.
