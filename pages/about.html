Our Story

GMA City Complex is a faith-based community dedicated to worship, counselling, healing and prayer. We believe that every person deserves access to spiritual guidance, emotional healing, and a supportive community that walks with them through life's challenges.

Our ministry is built on the foundation of God's Word, and we are committed to serving our members and community with love, compassion, and excellence. Through our various programs and services, we aim to restore hope, strengthen families, and transform lives.

✍

Our Mission

To reach out with love, strengthen families and walk with every believer on their spiritual journey.

Our Vision

To build a vibrant community where healing, prayer and godly counsel meet real everyday needs.
